# GT PSP cars for Gran Turismo 4 Spec II

![Bugatti Veyron 16.4 '09 in GT4 Photo Travel](screenshots/cover-veyron.png)

33 cars from *Gran Turismo PSP* are now in *Gran Turismo 4 Spec II v1.10*. The Veyron, Enzo, F2007, Countach, Furai, GT by Citroën and more can be bought and driven in GT Mode.

Ferrari, Lamborghini and Bugatti have their own dealers and tune shops. They're also in Arcade Mode's **By Make** list. This is a patch for **Spec II v1.10 NTSC-U only**.

## New in v1.0.1

The ported cars now have their own headlights and tail lamps at night:
- **Headlights** with the same halo as GT4's own cars.
- **Position and brake lamps** sit on each car's tail lights, plus a third brake light where the car has one.
- **Light on the road** in front of each headlight. The GT-R SpecV had none before.

In v1.0.0 the cars borrowed these from a GT4 donor car, so they sat in the wrong places.

![Enzo headlights and GT-R brake lamps on Special Stage Route 5](screenshots/night-lamps.jpg)

Of the 33 entries, 20 use car models converted from GT PSP, with their wheels and colours. The other 13 are PSP variants built on matching GT4 bodies. Some entries are special-colour versions. The full list is in the [release notes](https://github.com/Memetrix/gt4-psp-cars-spec-ii/releases/latest).

You need your own clean *GT4 Spec II v1.10 NTSC-U* ISO. The download contains an xdelta patch, not an ISO. The clean ISO's MD5 is `2c89bc11ccf68dc68f8af39c9e75096a`. [Delta Patcher](https://github.com/marco-calautti/DeltaPatcher/releases) applies it on Windows and macOS.

## Current limits

Engine sounds use the closest available GT4 donors. GT PSP has no night, so the lamp lenses themselves don't light up (the halo carries the light), and the pop-up headlights of the Countach and RX-7 stay closed. The Veyron's wing is static, and the new cars aren't in By Era or the existing AI lineups. Dealer and tune-shop photos are generated art; the screenshots above are from the game.
